IELTS Study Suite

100 free study apps for IELTS Academic and General Training: Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking and vocabulary. No sign-up and no ads. Progress stays in your browser. Every app is also a Windows program on GitHub.

All questions and recordings were written for this suite; none are official IELTS material. Listening uses your device's voices. Not endorsed by or affiliated with the British Council, IDP or Cambridge University Press & Assessment. IELTS is a registered trademark of its owners.

Listening

Form Completion TrainerHear Part 1 style calls about bookings and registrations, and fill in names, numbers, dates and prices. Every call is generated, so you never run out.Spelled Names and CodesHear names, postcodes, reference codes and email addresses spelled letter by letter, and type them exactly.Numbers, Dates and TimesHear phone numbers, prices, dates, times and years, including the tricky ones such as 15 and 50 and double six.Part 2 TalksListen to talks about everyday places and events, such as a museum tour or a sports club, and answer Part 2 style questions.Part 3 DiscussionsListen to students and tutors discussing assignments, and answer multiple-choice and matching questions.Part 4 LecturesListen to short academic lectures and complete the notes with one word only, as in Part 4.Map and Plan LabellingFollow spoken directions round a generated map or floor plan, and label each place with its letter.Distractor TrainerSpeakers change their minds and correct each other. Learn to catch the final answer, not the first one you hear.Word Limit RulesPractise the instructions such as NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER, and check whether an answer breaks them.Listening MatchingMatch people, places or options to what speakers say about them, as in Parts 2 and 3.Dictation TrainerHear a sentence, type it, and see word by word what you missed.Different Voices TrainerHear the same sentences in every English voice on your computer, at different speeds, as IELTS uses a range of accents.Lecture SignpostsLearn the phrases lecturers use to move between points, so you always know where the next answer is.Answer PredictionBefore you listen, predict what kind of word fits each gap: a number, a plural noun, a name or an adjective.Listening Test SimulatorA full Listening practice test: four parts and 40 questions, heard once, with 2 minutes to check at the end, as on computer.Listening Topic WordsHear and spell the words that come up again and again in IELTS Listening: accommodation, transport, health, study and more.Spelling PitfallsHear and type the words test takers misspell most, such as accommodation, environment and necessary. A wrong spelling is a wrong answer.Sentence Completion ListeningHear a short extract and complete a sentence about it with the right words from what you heard.Speed ListeningHear the same line slow, normal and fast, and train your ear for natural speech.Paraphrase SpottingIELTS rarely repeats the words in the question. Hear a sentence and choose the option that says the same thing differently.

Reading

True, False or Not GivenDecide whether statements agree with a text, contradict it, or are not mentioned, with the reason for every answer.Yes, No or Not GivenDecide whether statements match the writer's views or claims, a question type about opinions rather than facts.Matching HeadingsChoose the heading that sums up each paragraph, and learn to ignore headings that only match one detail.Matching InformationFind which paragraph contains a given piece of information, such as an example, a comparison or a reason.Matching FeaturesMatch statements to the researchers, places or dates they belong to, in passages that mention several of each.Matching Sentence EndingsComplete each sentence with the ending that matches the passage, where several endings are grammatical but only one is true.Summary CompletionComplete summaries of a passage, from a box of words or with words from the text.Short-Answer QuestionsAnswer questions with no more than three words from the passage, spelled exactly as in the text.Flow-Chart and Diagram CompletionRead descriptions of processes and complete the flow-chart or the labels of a diagram.Academic Reading PracticeLong academic passages with a mix of question types, timed at 20 minutes each, as in the Academic test.Everyday Texts (General Training)Notices, adverts, timetables and leaflets with General Training Section 1 style questions.Workplace Texts (General Training)Job adverts, staff handbooks and training notices with General Training Section 2 style questions.Skimming TrainerRead a paragraph against the clock and pick its main idea, to build the speed IELTS Reading needs.Scanning TrainerFind names, dates and numbers in a passage as fast as you can. Questions are made from the passages, so there are over a hundred.Synonym SpottingMatch the words in questions to the different words the passage uses for the same idea.Reading Speed TrainerMeasure your reading speed with IELTS-style passages, check understanding, and watch your speed grow.Reading Test SimulatorA full Reading practice test: three sections and 40 questions in 60 minutes, in Academic or General Training form.Reference WordsWork out what this, it, they and such refer to, a skill behind many wrong answers in Reading.Multiple-Choice ReadingMultiple-choice questions on passages, including choose TWO, where every wrong option is wrong for a reason.Reading Strategy GuideA plan for every Reading question type: what to read first, the usual traps, and a short practice set.

Writing

The overview is what examiners look for first.Task 1 Letters (General Training)Write formal, semi-formal and informal letters in 20 minutes, covering all three bullet points.Letter Tone TrainerChoose the right opening, closing and phrasing for formal, semi-formal and informal letters.Task 2 Essay WriterWrite a Task 2 essay of at least 250 words in 40 minutes, with original prompts of every type.Essay Type SpotterDecide whether a prompt asks for an opinion, a discussion, problems and solutions, or advantages and disadvantages.Task 2 PlannerPlan an essay in five minutes with the right structure for its type, then write from your plan.Introduction TrainerChoose and write introductions that paraphrase the prompt and give a clear position.Body Paragraph BuilderPut topic sentences, explanations and examples in order to build well-organised paragraphs.Cohesion TrainerLinking words and reference words that join ideas without overusing however and moreover.Writing Self-CheckCheck your essay against the four things examiners judge, with plain-language questions for each.Common Writing ErrorsFind and fix the mistakes that cost marks most often: agreement, articles, tenses and word choice.Complex Sentence BuilderJoin simple sentences with relative clauses, conditionals and participles, for a wider range of structures.
